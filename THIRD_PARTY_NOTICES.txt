Bundled MobileNetV3-Small model
------------------------------

File: models/mobilenet_v3_small.onnx
Source architecture: torchvision 0.24.1 mobilenet_v3_small
Source weights: MobileNet_V3_Small_Weights.IMAGENET1K_V1
Source checkpoint:
https://download.pytorch.org/models/mobilenet_v3_small-047dcff4.pth
Architecture source:
https://github.com/pytorch/vision/blob/v0.24.1/torchvision/models/mobilenetv3.py

The export retains the pretrained feature extractor and global average pool,
removes the classification head, and flattens the output to 576 features. It
uses ONNX opset 17, float32 tensors, and a dynamic batch dimension.

ONNX SHA-256:
